#  ScamGuard

**AI-powered scam detection for a safer digital world.**

ScamGuard is a cybersecurity application that uses Large Language Models (LLMs) to help users identify potential scams and fraudulent activity. From suspicious links and phishing messages to fraudulent bank accounts and phone numbers, ScamGuard brings multiple scam detection tools into one accessible platform.

The goal is simple: make digital safety easier to understand and accessible to everyone, regardless of their age or technical knowledge.

##  Key Features

- **Multi-Input Scam Detection** — Analyze phone numbers, bank accounts, URLs, and suspicious messages to assess potential risks.
- **LLM-Powered Analysis** — Use AI to examine suspicious content, identify potential red flags, and provide context for its assessment.
- **Image & Screenshot Analysis** — Inspect screenshots and images that may contain phishing attempts, fraudulent messages, or other scam indicators.
- **Scam Database Integration** — Check supported inputs against known-risk data to help identify potentially suspicious information.
- **User-Friendly Interface** — Designed to make scam detection approachable for teenagers, adults, and older users.

##  Project Mission

Scams are becoming increasingly difficult to recognize, especially when attackers impersonate trusted organizations or exploit people's trust.

ScamGuard explores how **Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models, and cybersecurity** can work together to help people recognize potential threats before acting on suspicious information.

This project is built around one principle:

> Stop. Check. Understand. Then trust.

##  Core Focus

- Cybersecurity & Fraud Prevention
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- AI-Assisted Threat Analysis
- Phishing Detection
- Digital Safety & User Awareness

##  Disclaimer

ScamGuard is intended to assist with scam awareness and risk assessment. Its results are not definitive proof that something is fraudulent or safe. Users should independently verify suspicious information before making financial or security-related decisions.
